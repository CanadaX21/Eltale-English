# Changelog

## 1.1.1

- **Battle camera:** no more wobble when you walk around a fight. The Japanese release aims its battle camera in 22.5-degree
  steps, and my faster camera follow made each step show. It now follows smoothly, at up to 3 degrees a frame. L, R and Z work as before.
- **Help setting:** **none** now also hides the spirit found counter on the Elements page.
- **Z-target gem heights** reviewed in play for all 75 monsters, bosses included.
- Older saves still work.

## 1.1

- **Help setting** on the Environment page: none, middle (the default) or full, for the Map pages' spirit counter and gems. It saves
  with your game. Older saves start on the middle.
- **Fixed:** at the Message slider's max setting, messages got stuck at their end, and a full last line added a blank page.
- **Battle drops** are kept until you hold 5 of an item (3 in the Japanese release).
- **Z-target gem heights** set for many more monsters.
- **Environment page:** B backs out of the rows as A does, and the min, max, none and full labels share one font and line up.
- Older saves still work.

## 1.0

The first full release.

- The complete English translation: dialogue, signs, boss speeches, the ending, the epilogue scroll, the backstory
  pages, menus and save screens.
- Modern controls: the item menu on B (R in the Japanese release), confirm on A only, the D-pad, the field camera (Z snap,
  R rotate, L zoom), Z-targeting in battle with a lock-on camera and side views, and a steadier battle camera.
- Brian keeps his heading after a battle, whether you win or run.
- A new item menu with fixed slots and counts.
- New Map page maps for seven areas, plus a spirit tracker on every map, the Status page and a redesigned Elements
  page.
- 31 new things to examine, and a save point at Kelly's house in Glencoe Forest.
- A few gameplay changes from the Japanese release: spell MP costs, MP from staff hits, reusable Wings, Soul Searcher, and
  which items open some doors. They are listed in the README.
